Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we receive most often at the Chancery.

To arrange a Catholic marriage in our Diocese, you should contact your parish priest at least six months before your intended wedding date. You will need to complete a Pre-Marriage Enquiry Form, attend a Pre-Cana (Marriage Preparation) course, and provide the required documents including baptismal certificates and confirmation certificates. If either party was previously married or has other special circumstances, additional steps may apply. Please use the contact form above or speak directly to your parish priest.
Baptismal and Confirmation certificates are held by the parish where the sacrament was administered. Contact the parish priest or parish secretary/catechist of that church directly, with details of the date of the sacrament and your full name at the time. If you are unsure which parish it was, you can also contact the Diocesan Chancery for assistance in locating the correct parish records.
Catholic parish boundaries are usually defined by geographic area. You are generally considered to belong to the territorial parish that covers the area where you live. However, you are also free to attend Mass and become an active member of any parish you choose. Visit our Parish Directory to browse parishes by deanery, or contact the Chancery for guidance on your territorial parish.
The Diocese of Okigwe is committed to the safety and wellbeing of all persons, especially children and vulnerable adults. Concerns about the conduct of a priest, deacon, religious, or Church worker may be reported directly to dean of the deanery orthe Vicar General at the Diocesan Chancery, or via the contact form on this page (select "Concern / Complaint" as your subject). All reports are treated confidentially and in accordance with the Church's Safeguarding policies and Nigerian law.
We welcome enquiries from young men and women discerning a vocation to priesthood or religious life. Please contact the Diocesan Vocations Director at jnwokeocha@gmail.com. You may also speak with your parish priest, who can accompany you in your discernment and put you in contact with the Vocations Director.
