Bishop Convokes Diocesan Synod on Evangelisation for 2025

🗓 July 1, 2025 ✍ Diocesan Communications

In a landmark pastoral letter addressed to the clergy, religious, and faithful of the Diocese of Okigwe, the Bishop has announced the convocation of a Diocesan Synod on the New Evangelisation, to be held in October 2025. The synodal process — inspired by the global Synod on Synodality called by Pope Francis — will gather delegates from all 48 parishes and 8 deaneries to discern the pastoral priorities of the Diocese for the next five years.
