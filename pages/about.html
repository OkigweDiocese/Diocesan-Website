Who We Are

The Diocese of Okigwe

The Catholic Diocese of Okigwe is a suffragan diocese of the Ecclesiastical Province of Owerri, located in Imo State in south-eastern Nigeria. Erected from its mother diocese, it encompasses a vibrant community of parishes, mission stations, schools, hospitals, and Catholic institutions spread across the region.

The Diocese is committed to the evangelisation of its people, the provision of quality Catholic education, and active participation in the social development of the communities it serves. Under the pastoral care of the Bishop of Okigwe, the faithful are formed, nourished, and sent forth as witnesses of Christ's love.

The Diocese belongs to the Catholic Bishops' Conference of Nigeria (CBCN) and works in communion with the Holy See and the universal Church. It maintains active relationships with Caritas Internationalis, Catholic Relief Services (CRS), and other Catholic development partners.

Our Journey

Diocesan History

A chronicle of faith, growth, and God's providence in Okigwe.

Pre-1950s

The Seeds of Faith

Catholic missionaries first brought the faith to the Okigwe region through the Holy Ghost Fathers (Spiritans). Early mission stations were established, schools were founded, and the first generation of local Catholics was formed. The Church became deeply embedded in the social fabric of the communities.

1961–1970

Ecclesiastical Growth

Following Nigerian independence in 1960, the Catholic Church in the region experienced remarkable growth in parishes, schools, and vocations. The Owerri Diocese expanded its pastoral reach into the Okigwe area, laying foundations for a future independent diocese.

Late 1970s

Preparations for a New Diocese

As the Catholic population in the Okigwe area grew substantially, discussions with the Holy See began regarding the establishment of a new diocese. New parishes were created, more priests were ordained, and the institutional capacity of the Church was strengthened.

Erection

Diocese of Okigwe Established

The Holy See, through a Papal Bull, officially established the Diocese of Okigwe as a suffragan of the Province of Owerri. The first Bishop was appointed and enthroned, marking a new chapter in the history of the Catholic Church in south-eastern Nigeria.

Present Day

A Thriving Diocesan Church

Today, the Diocese of Okigwe is a vibrant, growing local Church with dozens of parishes, a rich network of Catholic schools and hospitals, active lay movements, and a strong commitment to evangelisation, education, and integral human development.

The Bishop

The Shepherd of Our Diocese

The Bishop of Okigwe Diocese exercises the fullness of the apostolic ministry in the Diocese, teaching, sanctifying, and governing the faithful entrusted to his care. He leads the Diocese in fidelity to the Gospel and in communion with the Holy Father.

As the chief shepherd of the local Church, the Bishop ordains priests and deacons, confirms the faithful, consecrates sacred spaces, and serves as the principal teacher and pastor of the Diocese.
