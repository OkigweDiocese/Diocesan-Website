Chancellors

The Chancellor is the chief notary and archivist of the Diocese, responsible for maintaining official diocesan records, authenticating documents, and managing the diocesan secretariat and correspondence.

Office of the

Financial Administrators

The Diocesan Financial Administrator (Econome) administers the temporal goods and financial resources of the Diocese under the Bishop's authority, ensuring proper stewardship, accountability, and transparency in all diocesan financial matters.

👤 Portrait Current

Financial Administrator
