About the Diocese of Okigwe

Rooted in faith, guided by tradition, and called to serve.

✦

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, administer the Sacraments, and build a community of disciples who serve God and humanity with justice, compassion, and integrity.

✦

Our Vision

A Diocese fully alive in faith — where every Catholic is an active witness of Christ's love, the poor are uplifted, and all people encounter the saving grace of God.

Featured Parishes

Discover the vibrant parish communities that form the heart of our Diocese.

Cathedral

Immaculate Conception Cathedral, Okigwe

📍 Okigwe, Imo State

Immaculate Conception Cathedral, Okigwe is the Mother Church of our diocese. It stands as the spiritual heart of the Diocese, where the faithful gather for the celebration of the Sacred Liturgy, diocesan events, and major ecclesial celebrations. Though still under construction, the Cathedral is a visible testament to the Diocese's faith, unity, and commitment to building a lasting house of God for future generations.

St. Mary's Pro-Cathedral Parish, Okigwe

📍 Okigwe, Imo State

St. Mary's Pro-Cathedral serves as the Cathedral seat of the Diocese. It is a centre of worship, formation, and community life at the heart of Okigwe town. The parish is known for its vibrant liturgical celebrations, strong youth ministry, and active social outreach programs that serve the wider community.

Visit Parish →

St. Columbas' Catholic Parish Agbaghara Nsu

📍 Ehime Mbano, Imo State

St. Columbas' Agbaghara Nsu is a vibrant parish community located in the heart of Ehime Mbano, which was founded in 1917. The parish serves as a spiritual home for hundreds of faithful in the area and continues to be a beacon of faith and service in the Diocese of Okigwe.

St. Mary's Catholic Parish Amuzi Obowo

📍 Obowo, Imo State

St. Mary's Catholic Parish Obowo is one of our vibrant parishes in Obowo West Deanery, serving a large and active Catholic community. The parish is known for its strong devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary, dynamic liturgical celebrations, and robust social outreach programs that support the local community.

News & Events

The latest from our parishes, programmes, and diocesan community.

Anniversary
April 28, 2026

Bishop Solomon A. Amatu Celebrates his Episcopal Silver Jubilee

The Catholic Diocese of Okigwe joyfully celebrated the Episcopal Silver Jubilee of her Chief Shepherd, Most Rev. Solomon A. Amatu, marking twenty-five years of faithful and dedicated episcopal ministry. The historic occasion brought together bishops, priests, religious, government officials, and the lay faithful in thanksgiving to God for the gift of Bishop Amatu's leadership, pastoral zeal, and unwavering commitment to the growth of the Church. The celebration was marked by a solemn Eucharistic celebration, heartfelt tributes, and prayers for God's continued blessings upon the Bishop as he continues to shepherd the people of God with wisdom, humility, and fatherly care.

Event
May 24, 2026

Bishop Solomon A. Amatu Gifts Assumption Parish Ukwunwangwu a Car

The parish priest and parishioners of Assumption Parish, Ukwunwangwu-Uturu celebrated this year's Feast of Pentecost in grand style, as they welcomed the presence of Most Rev. Solomon Amatu, the Catholic Bishop of Okigwe. The bishop, who came on a surprise extraordinary pastoral visit to the parish, celebrated the holy mass with the faithful. As part of his extraordinary surprise package for the visit, the bishop made a donation of one of his official cars to the parish for the enhancement of the ongoing work of evangelization in the parish.

Cathedraticum
April 01, 2026

Okigwe Deanery Celebrates Cathedraticum 2026 with the Bishop

Okigwe Deanery joyfully celebrated the 2026 Cathedraticum with His Lordship, Most Rev. Solomon A. Amatu, at St. Mary's Pro-Cathedral Parish, Okigwe. The annual celebration brought together priests, religious, and lay faithful of the deanery in a spirit of thanksgiving, unity, and communion with their Bishop. In his homily, Bishop Amatu encouraged the faithful to remain steadfast in faith and committed to the mission of the Church. At the end, he imparted the Papal Blessing from Rome on them. The event was marked by vibrant liturgical celebrations and renewed expressions of ecclesial solidarity within the Diocese.
