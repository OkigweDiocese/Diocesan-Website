About the Diocese of Okigwe

Rooted in faith, guided by tradition, and called to serve.

A BRIEF HISTORY

of the Catholic Church in Okigwe Diocese

Okigwe Catholic Diocese was evangelized by the missionaries from two wings namely: Adazi in Anambra State and Emekuku in Owerri (Imo state). While Okigwe North was evangelized from Adazi, Okigwe South was taken care of from Emekuku. Consequently, they created two Missions: Holy Cross Mission, Uturu (1912) and St. Columbas' Mission Nsu (1917). From these two Missions, the Catholic faith sprouted and began to grow into what we have today as Okigwe Diocese. The Church in the zone continued to grow from strength to strength under the able leadership of Bishop A. G. Nwedo of Umuahia Diocese.

On January 24, 1981, His Holiness Pope John Paul II carved out Okigwe zone from Umuahia Diocese and elevated it into a diocese of its own. Most Rev. Anthony Ekezia Ilonu who was appointed by the Holy Father as the pioneer bishop was consecrated on March 29, 1981. Bishop A. E. Ilonu diligently pastured this diocese for 25 years. Briefly before his retirement, the then Auxiliary Bishop of Awka, Most Rev. Solomon Amanchukwu Amatu was appointed the Coadjutor Bishop of Okigwe Diocese on July 16, 2005. Bishop S. A. Amatu took over as the diocesan Bishop of the diocese at the retirement of Bishop Ilonu on April 22, 2006 and was installed on July 1, 2006.

The Diocese is currently blessed with 134 parishes, 19 parishes-in-building and 9 chaplaincies. She has 550 priests, 128 major seminarians and 507 minor seminarians. Serving in the Diocese are 38 Religious Men and 257 Religious women of different religious congregations. The Diocese is dedicated to Our Lady, Immaculate Conception.

Cathedral

St. Mary's Pro-Cathedral Parish Okigwe

📍 Okigwe, Imo State

St. Mary's Pro-Catheral serves as the Cathedral seat of the Diocese. It is a centre of worship, formation, and community life at the heart of Okigwe town. The parish is known for its vibrant liturgical celebrations, strong youth ministry, and active social outreach programs that serve the wider community.

St. Columbas' Catholic Parish Agbaghara Nsu

📍 Ehime Mbano, Imo State

St. Columbas' Agbaghara Nsu is a vibrant parish community located in the heart of Ehime Mbano, which was founded in 1917. The parish serves as a spiritual home for hundreds of faithful in the area and continues to be a beacon of faith and service in the Diocese of Okigwe.

St. Mary's Catholic Parish Amuzi Obowo

📍 Obowo, Imo State

St. Mary's Catholic Parish Obowo is one of our vibrant parishes in Obowo West Deary, serving a large and active Catholic community. St. Chareles Catholic Parish Umunumo

📍 Ehime Mbano, Imo State

St.Charles Parish Umunumo is a mother church. It was established in 1928 and has been a spiritual home for generations of Catholics in the area. The parish is known for its vibrant liturgical celebrations, strong community spirit, and active involvement in social justice initiatives that uplift the local community.

Anniversary
April 28, 2026

Bishop Solomon A. Amatu Celebrates his Episcopal Silver Jubilee

The Catholic Diocese of Okigwe joyfully celebrated the Episcopal Silver Jubilee of her Chief Shepherd, Most Rev. Solomon A. Amatu, marking twenty-five years of faithful and dedicated episcopal ministry. The historic occasion brought together bishops, priests, religious, government officials, and the lay faithful in thanksgiving to God for the gift of Bishop Amatu's leadership, pastoral zeal, and unwavering commitment to the growth of the Church. The celebration was marked by a solemn Eucharistic celebration, heartfelt tributes, and prayers for God's continued blessings upon the Bishop as he continues to shepherd the people of God with wisdom, humility, and fatherly care.

Event
May 24, 2026

Bishop Solomon A. Amatu Gifts Assumption Parish Ukwunwangwu a Car

The parish priest and parishioners of Assumption Parish, Ukwunwangwu-Uturu celebrated this year's Feast of Pentecost in grand style, as they welcomed the presence of Most Rev. Solomon Amatu, the Catholic Bishop of Okigwe. The bishop, who came on a surprise extraordinary pastoral visit to the parish, celebrated the holy mass with the faithful. As part of his extraordinary surprise package for the visit, the bishop made a donation of one of his official cars to the parish for the enhancement of the ongoing work of evangelization in the parish.

Cathedraticum
April 01, 2026

Okigwe Deanery Celebrates Cathedraticum 2026 with the Bishop

Okigwe Deanery joyfully celebrated the 2026 Cathedraticum with His Lordship, Most Rev. Solomon A. Amatu, at St. Mary's Pro-Cathdral Parish, Okigwe. The annual celebration brought together priests, religious, and lay faithful of the deanery in a spirit of thanksgiving, unity, and communion with their Bishop. In his homily, Bishop Amatu encouraged the faithful to remain steadfast in faith and committed to the mission of the Church. At the end, he empacted the Papal Blessing from Rome on them. The event was marked by vibrant liturgical celebrations and renewed expressions of ecclesial solidarity within the Diocese.
